# Cruise Ship Repertoire: research for the PERSISTINT cruise chapter

Prepared for the PERSISTINT design team, November 2026.

Real material on large cruise ships, for choosing rooms, props, activities and mini-games for the feminist-themed celebrity cruise chapter.

**Citations.** Each fact carries the URL it came from, and every URL was fetched while preparing this report. **UNVERIFIED** marks anything we could not confirm on a fetched page. Several cruise sites blocked automated access (royalcaribbeanblog.com, cruisecritic.com, encyclopedia-titanica.org, seatrade-cruise.com), so a few well-known details, such as Royal Caribbean's game-show rules, appear only as UNVERIFIED.

Two further cautions:

- **Deck plans.** These come mostly from CruiseMapper. CruiseMapper says its plans are taken from the cruise lines' official PDFs but does not guarantee they are accurate.
- **Sections 6 and 7 are design synthesis.** They turn the cited facts into art and game guidance. Where an item comes directly from a source, that source is cited. Everything else in those two sections is a drawing or design suggestion, not a researched claim.

## 1. Deck plans

### 1.1 The general pattern on a modern mega-ship

Read from top to bottom, every large modern ship sorts into the same layers. Each layer below is confirmed on at least three of the five ships in 1.2.

1. **Topdeck and thrill deck:** waterslides, sundeck, sometimes a roller coaster or ropes course.
2. **Sports deck:** courts, mini golf, FlowRider, teen areas.
3. **Pool or "Lido" deck:** main pools, hot tubs, the buffet; the spa here or one deck below.
4. **Bridge:** forward, on a high cabin deck (Deck 12 on Wonder and Icon, 14 on Getaway, 16 on Mardi Gras, 15 on World Europa).
5. **Cabin decks:** five or more, mid-ship.
6. **Entertainment and promenade decks**, roughly Decks 3–8: atrium, promenade, theatre, casino, shops, comedy club, main dining.
7. **Lowest passenger deck:** tender boarding and the medical centre (Deck 2 on Wonder and Icon, Deck 4 on World Europa) ([Wonder](https://www.cruisemapper.com/deckplans/Wonder-Of-The-Seas-2165), [Icon](https://cruisemapper.com/deckplans/Icon-Of-The-Seas-2110), [World Europa](https://www.cruisemapper.com/deckplans/MSC-World-Europa-2183)).
8. **Crew decks.** The crew's main corridor, "I-95", usually sits on Deck 0 or 1, at or just below the waterline, linking crew messes, cabins, provisions, laundry, medical and engine spaces. Most crew cabins have no windows; entry-level ones are about 9 by 9 ft and shared ([cruiseshiptracking.com](https://www.cruiseshiptracking.com/blog/cruise-ship-below-deck-crew-areas)). On Explorer of the Seas, I-95 is on Deck 1, the main galley on Deck 3, laundry below the waterline, and provisions arrive by forklift through shell doors ([mycruisestories.com](https://mycruisestories.com/2018/07/13/behind-the-scenes-tour-on-explorer-of-the-seas/)).
9. **Below the waterline:** engine rooms, shaft tunnels, thrusters, stabilisers, sewage and desalination plants, food stores; the morgue is usually under the medical centre ([Chris Frame](https://chrisframe.com.au/post/722878745104482304)).

### 1.3 RMS Titanic (1912)

**Deck order, top to bottom:** Boat Deck; A (Promenade); B (Bridge); C (Shelter); D (Saloon); E (Upper); F (Middle); G (Lower); Orlop; Tank Top ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic)). The British inquiry report also lists a Lower Orlop ([Titanic Inquiry Project](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php)).

**What was on each deck:**

- **Boat Deck:** lifeboats (20 in all), the bridge, the top of the Grand Staircase and the gymnasium (starboard side) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic); [first-class facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_facilities_of_the_Titanic)).
- **A Deck:** the first-class promenade. Reading room, lounge, smoke room and Verandah Café ([Inquiry](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php); [first-class facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_facilities_of_the_Titanic)).
- **B Deck:** the à la carte restaurant and the Café Parisien.
- **C Deck:** the highest deck that ran unbroken from bow to stern. Most first-class cabins, plus the mail, specie and parcel room ([Inquiry](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php)).
- **D Deck:** the first-class dining saloon, called "the largest room afloat". It was 112 ft long and sat between the second and third funnels. The 83 ft reception room was next to it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic); [Inquiry](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php)).
- **E Deck:** all three classes, and the long working corridor that crew called "Scotland Road" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic)).
- **F Deck:** the swimming bath (a saltwater pool) and the Turkish baths. The sources disagree: Wikipedia puts them on F Deck ([RMS Titanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic); [first-class facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-class_facilities_of_the_Titanic)), but the inquiry report lists them under its E Deck heading ([Inquiry](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php)).
- **G Deck:** the squash (racquet) court and the post office.
- **Orlop:** cargo.
- **Tank Top:** boilers and engines.

**Stairs and lifts.** The Grand Staircase ran from the Boat Deck to E Deck under a wrought-iron and glass dome, then as a single flight to F Deck ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Staircase_of_the_Titanic)). Three first-class lifts ran from E Deck to A Deck, not to the Boat Deck; the second-class stair and lift ran from the Boat Deck to F Deck ([Inquiry](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepAccomo.php)).

**Holds and machinery.** Mail, parcel and specie space was 26,800 cu ft; first- and second-class baggage 19,455 cu ft ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Titanic)). 29 boilers in six watertight boiler rooms, then, moving aft, the reciprocating engine room and the turbine room ([Inquiry, machinery](https://www.titanicinquiry.org/BOTInq/BOTReport/botRepMach.php)).

### 1.4 SS United States (1952)

We chose SS United States because it is better sourced than Normandie.

- **Layout logic.** First-class public rooms sat midships, away from engine vibration. First and cabin class shared the pool, theatre and gym. 12 decks, up to 1,972 passengers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_United_States)).
- **Promenade Deck:** observation lounge, library, ballroom, cocktail bar, grill and smoking room ([Last Ocean Liners](https://lastoceanliners.com/line?l=USL)).
- **A Deck:** all the passenger restaurants, with a shared galley between the first- and cabin-class dining rooms ([SS United States Conservancy](https://www.ssusc.org/take-a-seat)).
- **Engines:** eight boilers in two separate engine rooms; four turbines, 240,000 shp; 38.32 knots on trials ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_United_States)). Chapel and theatre decks: UNVERIFIED.
- **Normandie alternative:** a 305 ft first-class dining room seating 700, plus a theatre, chapel, stepped pool and winter garden ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Normandie)); room decks UNVERIFIED.

## 2. The daily programme

### 2.1 What the programme is

Every ship issues a daily schedule, on paper or in an app:

- **Carnival FunTimes.** The front page gives port, dress code, comedy line-up, bingo time and the night's theme, then an hour-by-hour list from as early as 7 a.m. ([Cruise Radio](https://cruiseradio.net/carnival-fun-times/)). It moved to the HUB app in 2021 ([Cruise Radio](https://cruiseradio.net/carnival-cruise-line-fun-times-paper-version-hub-app-only/)).
- **P&O "Horizon".** Uses category icons: Family Fun, Dancing, Quiz, Films, Relaxing, Active, Learn Something New ([scanned Horizon, Ventura](https://www.beyondships2.com/uploads/8/2/4/5/8245255/po_ventura_sea.pdf)).

Royal Caribbean's Cruise Compass, Princess Patter and NCL's Freestyle Daily could not be fetched (image-only scans or blocked blogs). The schedules below come from real scanned programmes.

### 2.3 Named activities

#### Trivia

- **Format.** Trivia runs once or several times a day, mostly on sea days. Teams are usually capped at six. Common themes are music, film, geography and decades ([CruiseBooking](https://www.cruisebooking.com/articles/first-time-cruisers/cruise-ship-trivia-things-to-know)).
- **Prizes.** Small ones. Carnival's is a plastic "ship on a stick" (same source; [Cruise Radio](https://cruiseradio.net/9-experiences-not-to-miss-on-a-carnival-cruise/)).
- **Real quiz names.**
  - P&O: Theme Quiz – Alphabet, Brain Teaser, Battle of the Sexes, Music Master, Famous Faces, Musicalities, Syndicate Quiz ([Ventura](https://www.beyondships2.com/uploads/8/2/4/5/8245255/po_ventura_sea.pdf)).
  - Disney: Pixar, Ocean, TV Theme Songs and Imagineering trivia ([Disney](https://disneycruiselineblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Personal-Navigators-Magic-14N-EBPC-Day-2-At-Sea-20231204.pdf)).
- **Feminist or women's-history trivia: UNVERIFIED.** We found no fetched page describing a women's-history trivia night on a women's charter. The closest confirmed items are:
  - Fan-specific trivia on a Real Housewives fan cruise ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/real-housewives-fan-cruise-departing-fort-lauderdale-in-january-2024---cast-members-whitney-rose-ashley-darby-and-dolores-catania-are-set-to-sail-exclusive-301790969.html)).
  - Themed trivia on a Taylor Swift fan charter ([Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/taylor-swift-themed-cruise-taking-over-royal-caribbean-ship/120049)).
  - An activism panel on an Olivia sea day ([Curve](https://www.curvemag.com/blog/travel/cruise-entry-2/)).

  A women's-history trivia night would therefore be a design invention, though a well-grounded one.

#### Bingo

- **How a session runs.** Three to five games over 30–45 minutes, each on a different coloured card: a line, four corners, then a blackout (cover-all) ([Cruise with Leo](https://cruisewithleo.com/bingo-cruise/); [CruiseBooking](https://www.cruisebooking.com/articles/cruise-holidays/cruise-ship-bingo)).
- **Prices.** Cards come in packs: Royal Caribbean $29 for 3; Carnival $58 for 11 cards, 2 scratch cards and a dauber; NCL $49 for 3 ([Cruise with Leo](https://cruisewithleo.com/bingo-cruise/); [Cruzely](https://www.cruzely.com/everything-to-know-about-playing-bingo-on-a-cruise/)).
- **Rules.** Players must be 18 or older and the pot grows with the number of players; Carnival advertises jackpots of up to $5,000 ([Carnival](https://carnival.com.au/onboard/bingo)).
- **Jackpot rule.** Carnival's grand prize must be won within a set number of calls, given as 45 ([Cruise with Leo](https://cruisewithleo.com/bingo-cruise/)). Progressive jackpots can carry over between sessions ([CruiseBooking](https://www.cruisebooking.com/articles/cruise-holidays/cruise-ship-bingo)).
- **"Snowball" bingo.** P&O ran Snowball Jackpot Bingo twice on one sea day ([Ventura](https://www.beyondships2.com/uploads/8/2/4/5/8245255/po_ventura_sea.pdf)). The exact snowball rule (cover-all within N numbers, otherwise the pot rolls over) is UNVERIFIED as written by a cruise line. It is consistent with the Carnival 45-call rule above.
- **Big prizes.** Prizes can include a free cruise ([CruiseBooking](https://www.cruisebooking.com/articles/cruise-holidays/cruise-ship-bingo)). On Azamara Quest in 2023, a couple won a blackout jackpot that upgraded them to the owner's suite ([Cruise Mummy](https://www.cruisemummy.co.uk/jackpot-upgrade-cruise-passenger-shares-their-story-of-the-ultimate-bingo-upgrade-win/)).
- **On a women's charter.** An Olivia cruise ran bingo with a jackpot of about $2,200 ([Curve](https://www.curvemag.com/blog/travel/gay-gal-at-sea-day-seven/)).

#### Deck games

- **Shuffleboard** was improvised on deck as early as the 1830s; the court is 6 by 39 ft with 6-inch discs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuffleboard)).
- **Quoits.** A 1910 handbook lists deck quoits, shuffleboard and bull-board as free games run by the quartermaster ([GG Archives](https://www.ggarchives.com/OceanTravel/TravelGuide/22-GamesAtSea.html)). Deck tennis crosses tennis and quoits with a rope ring ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deck_tennis)). P&O still listed quoits, shuffleboard and table tennis in 2010 ([Ventura](https://www.beyondships2.com/uploads/8/2/4/5/8245255/po_ventura_sea.pdf)).
- **Cornhole and mini golf.** HAL's Sport Court has cornhole and pickleball ([HAL](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/us/onboard-experiences/activities)); every Carnival ship has mini golf with a cactus, treasure chest and windmill ([Carnival](https://carnival.com.au/onboard/mini-golf)).
- **Giant chess:** UNVERIFIED.

#### Pool games

- Carnival has retired its belly-flop and hairy-chest contests fleet-wide to make the pool deck more family-friendly ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/news/carnival-ends-bellyflop-hairy-chest)). Royal Caribbean's belly flop was judged on "style, grace, and self-inflicted pain" ([Porthole](https://porthole.com/the-belly-flop-competition-keys-to-victory/)).
- Olivia sea days have "Wet & Wacky pool games" ([Curve](https://www.curvemag.com/blog/travel/cruise-entry-2/)), the natural women's-charter version.

#### Karaoke and game shows

- **Karaoke.** On Carnival it is free, scheduled, open to all ages, with lyrics provided ([Carnival](https://carnival.com.au/onboard/karaoke)). P&O split it into an evening "Spotlight" session and "Late Night" karaoke ([Ventura](https://www.beyondships2.com/uploads/8/2/4/5/8245255/po_ventura_sea.pdf)).
- **Love and Marriage, Majority Rules, Quest: all UNVERIFIED.** Royal Caribbean's own pages did not render, and the blogs describing them blocked access. The commonly reported formats, from search snippets only, are below. Treat them as starting points for design, not as facts:
  - Love and Marriage: three couples chosen by how long they have been married, newlywed-game style.
  - Majority Rules: you score by matching the room's most common answer.
  - Quest: an adults-only late-night team scavenger hunt in which team captains fetch absurd items.
- **Fan game shows.** A Sixthman music charter ran Family Feud with artists against guests ([Mailchimp case study](https://mailchimp.com/resources/issue-55-featuring-sixthman/)).

#### Dance and parties

- **Dance classes.** Salsa, line dancing, ballroom and bachata appear on both schedules, and P&O's formal Gala Ball was hosted by the dance instructors (schedules above).
- **Carnival theme parties:** 80's Rock and Glow (glow necklaces), Motown, White Hot (all white under black lights), Silent Disco and the Mega Deck Party (beads, thunder sticks) ([Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-cruise-line-shares-list-of-theme-parties/105079)). They last 30–45 minutes and are led by the cruise director and "Fun Squad" ([ProfCruise](https://profcruise.com/carnival-theme-parties)).
- **Royal Caribbean theme nights:** 70s/80s, White Night, Neon, Caribbean and Latin ([Cruise with Leo](https://cruisewithleo.com/royal-theme-nights/)). NCL's Glow and White Hot parties: UNVERIFIED.
- **Sail-away party:** free, all ages, live DJ on deck as the ship leaves port ([Carnival](https://carnival.com.au/onboard/sailaway-party)).

#### Ship's rituals

- **Muster drill.** Now "eMuster". Guests review the safety video on their phone or cabin TV, then check in at their assembly station with a crew member before the ship sails. Royal Caribbean first tested it on Symphony of the Seas in January 2020 and licensed it to other lines ([Porthole](https://porthole.com/royal-caribbean-muster-safety-drill/)).
- **Formal night.** Carnival calls it "Cruise Elegant": two nights on cruises of six days or more. From August 2026 some 3–4 night cruises replace it with a White Hot Night deck party ([Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-scales-back-elegant-evening-on-shorter-sailings/215815); [Cruise Mummy](https://www.cruisemummy.co.uk/carnival-replaces-formal-night-with-white-hot-theme/)). Royal Caribbean now uses "Dress Your Best" ([Cruise with Leo](https://cruisewithleo.com/royal-theme-nights/)).

- **Champagne waterfall** (a tower of glasses filled from the top): UNVERIFIED.

#### Art, spa, towels and cooking

- **Art auctions.** Park West Gallery has run shipboard auctions since 1993 on most big lines, and faced class-action suits over valuations in 2008 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_West_Gallery)). ## 3. Food and drink

### 3.1 The buffet

- **Carnival Lido Marketplace:** BBQ, deli, salad, Asian, pasta and a daily "chef's surprise" station, plus pizza and soft serve ([Carnival](https://www.carnival.com/cruise-food/lido-marketplace)).
- **Holland America Lido Market:** stations named Bread Board, Wild Harvest, Homestead, Distant Lands, Roasting Pan and Sweet Spots ([HAL](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/us/onboard-experiences/dining/lido-market)).
- **Princess World Fresh Marketplace:** open 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., midnight on some itineraries ([Princess](https://www.princess.com/en-int/cruise-dining)). NCL's buffet is the Garden Café ([NCL](https://www.ncl.com/cruise-ships/norwegian-joy/whats-on-board/complimentary-dining)); Royal Caribbean's Windjammer on Oasis is laid out as themed food "islands" ([Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2009/06/62509-oasis-of-the-seas-introduces-next-generation-of-dining/)).
- **The midnight buffet and its decline.**
  - By 2007 the nightly midnight buffet was almost gone. Carnival kept a weekly "Grand Gala Buffet", for which chefs prepared 910 lb of food. Princess had dropped its buffet at least a decade earlier ([Spokesman-Review](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2007/nov/04/midnight-buffets-bite-the-dust)).
  - Causes cited: 24-hour pizza, 24-hour room service and alternative restaurants. One industry expert said simply that nobody was showing up (same source). Another 2007 report called it a relic ([TravelMole](https://www.travelmole.com/news/no-more-appetite-for-the-midnight-cruise-buffet)).
  - Carnival brought back a scaled-down version in 2024 ([Islands](https://www.islands.com/1987797/carnival-cruise-discontinued-experiences-activities-items-passenger-wish-comeback)).
- **Late-night food today.** 24-hour pizza is the main late-night option: Pizzeria del Capitano on Carnival ([Carnival](https://www.carnival.com/awaywego/cruising-fun/what-to-expect/the-9-best-restaurants-onboard-carnival-vista)), Sorrento's on Royal Caribbean ([Cruise Fever](https://cruisefever.net/free-places-to-eat-on-icon-of-the-seas-no-extra-cost/)). ## 4. Themed charters for women and LGBTQ+ groups

### 4.1 Olivia, the closest real model for the chapter

- **Origins.** Olivia Records was founded in 1973 in Washington, D.C., as a women's music label ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Records)). Olivia Travel ran its first all-women cruise in 1990 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Travel)). In 1989 founder Judy Dlugacz wrote to the label's mailing list offering a four-day Bahamas cruise for 600 women, and demand added a second sailing ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2023/08/14/women-were-able-to-be-themselves-for-the-first-time-inside-the-worlds-first-lesbian-travel)).
- **Getting a ship.** Cruise lines were reluctant to charter to lesbians. A Greek family-owned line was the first to agree ([Olivia](https://www.olivia.com/about-our-trips/legacy)).
- **Full-ship charters.** Olivia charters the whole ship, on cruise ships of up to about 2,200 passengers ([OutTraveler](https://www.outtraveler.com/print-issue/2022/8/31/olivia-revolutionized-travel-lesbians-heres-how)).
- **Largest sailing.** The 2025 "Coral Jubilee" carried more than 2,600 guests on Holland America's Nieuw Statendam. Olivia billed it as the largest LGBTQ+ women's cruise ([Olivia](https://www.olivia.com/about-our-trips/legacy); [Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/olivia-travel-35-years-cruise/)).
- **Theme nights on the 2025 sailing.** Prom Night, Under the Sea Night, White Night, Hometown Day, Pajama Night and Rainbow Pride Day. Other sailings have had a White Party, "Show Your Roots" (dress for your hometown), "Shero" (dress as a female hero) and a Black and White Ball ([Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/olivia-travel-35-years-cruise/); [Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/dames-sea); [Curve](https://www.curvemag.com/blog/travel/jenny-hits-the-high-seas-on-olivia-day-two/)).
- **Daily programme.** Dance lessons, theme nights, guest speakers, karaoke, meet-ups, colouring and dance parties ([Olivia](https://olivia.com/about-our-trips/activities)); a nightly dance party, "Club O", in the top-deck lounge ([Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/dames-sea)); an author talk and a spoken-word open mic ([Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/olivia-travel-35-years-cruise/)); deck Scrabble and bingo, a morning greeting to the "Ladies of Olivia", and a theme-song sing-along before shows ([Curve](https://www.curvemag.com/blog/travel/gay-gal-at-sea-day-seven/)).
- **Affinity groups.** Solos; Gen-O for women under 40, with pool games and karaoke ([Olivia](https://olivia.com/gen-o)); OWLs ("Older, Wiser Lesbians"); 12-Step support; and Trans & Nonbinary ([Olivia](https://olivia.com/about-our-trips/activities); [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/shannonkeating/lesbian-cruise)).
- **Message board.** One sailing had a public board for personal ads ([BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/shannonkeating/lesbian-cruise)).
- **Speakers and stars.** The 2014 Equality and Leadership Summit cruise had Maya Angelou as keynote, with the Indigo Girls and Judy Gold performing ([Girls That Roam](https://girlsthatroam.com/?p=2763)). Past trips included leadership voyages with Billie Jean King and tennis clinics with Martina Navratilova ([OutTraveler](https://www.outtraveler.com/print-issue/2022/8/31/olivia-revolutionized-travel-lesbians-heres-how)). Performers have included Kate Clinton, Melissa Etheridge and Wynonna ([Windy City Times](https://windycitytimes.com/2004/12/08/travel-whats-to-do-on-a-sea-cruise/)).

### 4.2 Other women's and LGBTQ+ charters

- **Sweet** (founded 2008): lesbian travel built on "voluntourism", with guests planting trees and clearing beaches in port ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_(company))).
- **RSVP** ran the first all-gay cruise in 1986 ([Banderas News](https://www.banderasnews.com/1003/to-rsvpvacations.htm)); **R Family Vacations**, co-founded by Rosie O'Donnell, first sailed in 2004 for LGBTQ+ families ([Advocate](https://www.advocate.com/news/2003/07/30/o39donnell-launches-r-family-vacatioins-9430)).
- **Atlantis Events** has chartered ships since 1998 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantis_Events)). Its named parties include the Dog Tag T-Dance (souvenir dog tags), the White Party and Think Pink ([Atlantis](https://atlantisevents.com/vacation/2025-valiant-memorialbreak/booked)). On one sailing, doors carried flags, occupant photos and message whiteboards, and the daily newsletter came with an art card for that night's party ([Travel Update](https://travelupdate.com/atlantis-cruises/)).
- **Vacaya.** Gave out colour-coded lanyards at boarding (red taken, yellow complicated, green available). Its finale party asked guests to wear one piece from every costume of the week ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/cruise/my-first-gay-cruise/)).

### 4.3 Celebrity, fan, podcast, comedy and literary sailings

- **Sixthman** (music festivals at sea, owned by NCL, more than 240 sailings by 2026 ([Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/06/sixthman-25-years-of-rocking-the-high-seas/))). - **Limitless Voyage (2023).** Curated by Jennifer Lopez on Virgin's Scarlet Lady and billed as "designed by women for women". It had 14 women speakers, panels, fireside chats and sound baths ([Virgin Voyages](https://www.virginvoyages.com/press/latest-releases/limitless-voyage-speakers)).
- **Wives at Sea (2024).** A Real Housewives fan cruise billed as "the ultimate girls trip": cast meet-and-greets, panels, Housewives trivia, bar crawls, costume contests and drag shows ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/real-housewives-fan-cruise-departing-fort-lauderdale-in-january-2024---cast-members-whitney-rose-ashley-darby-and-dolores-catania-are-set-to-sail-exclusive-301790969.html)).
- **Taylor Swift fan charter (2024).** An unofficial full charter with bracelet swaps, themed bingo and door-decorating contests ([Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/taylor-swift-themed-cruise-taking-over-royal-caribbean-ship/120049)).
- **Drag Stars at Sea (2012, Carnival)** carried more than 30 Drag Race performers ([Reality Blurred](https://www.realityblurred.com/realitytv/2012/11/rupauls-drag-race-drag-stars-sea-carnival/)).
- **Comedy and podcasts.** Nate Bargatze's 2026 charter had about 18 comics, podcast tapings and a belly-flop contest ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/02/05/nate-bargatze-takes-to-the-seas-with-new-cruise/)); Virgin and iHeart's podcast cruises begin in October 2026 ([Caribbean Journal](https://www.caribjournal.com/2026/04/12/virgin-voyages-podcast-cruises/)).
- **Literary.** Cunard's Literature Festival at Sea on Queen Mary 2 was run with the Cheltenham Literature Festival, with authors including Bernardine Evaristo and Mary Beard ([Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2022/02/queen-mary-2-will-host-the-2022-literature-festival-at-sea)).
- **Theme nights as tradition.** The ShipRocked rock charter holds one costume theme every night ([ShipRocked](https://shiprocked.com/theme-nights)).
- **Not found (UNVERIFIED):** Oprah, O Magazine or Glamour cruises; a Bravo fan cruise (only a one-off talk-show taping is confirmed); a My Favorite Murder cruise.

### 4.4 What makes a charter different from a mainstream sailing

1. **The whole ship is bought out**, and the charter's programme replaces the line's ([Olivia](https://dallasvoice.com/making-womens-history); [Atlantis](https://atlantisevents.com/vacation/2025-valiant-memorialbreak/booked)).
2. **A nightly costume theme**, which the whole ship dresses for (Olivia, Atlantis, ShipRocked above).
3. **Headliners mix with guests:** Q&As, photo ops, game shows against the artists (Etheridge, Sixthman).
4. **Daytime panels and workshops matter as much as the nights** ([Dallas Voice](https://dallasvoice.com/dames-sea)).
5. **Affinity meet-ups** (solos, under-40s, older women, sober guests) and **community rituals**: the theme song, the morning greeting, message boards.
6. **Decorated cabin doors.** Steel doors take magnets ([Eye of the Flyer](https://eyeoftheflyer.com/2023/04/11/why-do-so-many-cruise-line-passengers-decorate-their-cabin-doors-with-magnets-and-more/)). Disney cruisers hang a "fish extender" gift pouch ([Cruise Radio](https://cruiseradio.net/cruise-door-decorations-what-you-need-to-know/)). Most lines now allow magnets only, and Virgin discourages decorations ([Your Mileage May Vary](https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/06/29/cruise-lines-are-cracking-down-on-a-tradition-many-cruisers-love/)).
7. **Branding everywhere:** the charter's logo on cups, napkins and lifts (Sixthman).
