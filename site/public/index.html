PERSISTINT is a free companion game: a small 2D game made in Godot 4. It wraps social control in the cutest possible packaging, so that players, and girls and women above all, can recognize it, name it and feel comfortable criticizing it, at school, at work or online.

The cuteness is the weapon.

Three worlds

The game moves between three worlds. In each one the player does the same things: dresses up, makes things, talks to people, goes places. Only the social framing changes, and that contrast is the lesson.

  1. Innocent play

    The tropical Florida Keys

    A Toca World / Animal Crossing sandbox with gardening, crafting, dolphins, friendly crabs and seagulls, aquarium-show mermaids and a pony stable. No scoring, and nothing can be done wrong.

  2. Judged performance

    The Cruise

    A feminist-themed celebrity cruise before the wreck. WarioWare-style mini-games hosted by real celebrities, where passengers are made over, judged and “empowered”.

    Flashbacks and a later chapter.

  3. Ritualized control

    Little St. Piere Island

    Long after the wreck, the ship and an old colony bunker are sacred ruins. A Covenant of Mothers and Priestesses runs daily life by the 28-day Calendar of Blood, and everyone contributes.

The same paper-doll dress-up is kind play in tropical Florida, a judged makeover on the cruise and part of the ritual on the island.

Name It

After a manipulative moment comes Name It: the player picks out the hook hidden in a compliment and names the tactic.

  • negging
  • concern-trolling
  • in-group gatekeeping

Named tactics go into a journal of Forbidden Knowledge.

There is no combat anywhere. Influence, not violence.

Cruise mini-games

  • The Interview

    Oprah Winfrey interviews you and your answers set your stats: character creation as a talk show.

  • The Landing

    Spaceteam-style panic on the bridge as Cap'n Kate tries not to crash a dead ship, with Among Us-flavored crew tasks.

  • Hottest of the HOT

    Tyra Banks's judging show, with Day of the Tentacle-style get-ready chains across the ship.

  • America's Next Top Comic

    A stand-up contest built like Monkey Island's insult sword fighting, and the player is the contestant. There is no winning.

Feeling some kind of way?

PERSISTINT wraps social control in the cutest possible packaging, and it is meant to sting a little. If a compliment in the game felt like a hook, or an island rule felt uncomfortably familiar, that reaction is worth paying attention to. You don't have to work out what to make of it alone.
