# Render hold

No prompt is to be rendered from this queue. Renders of these prompts arriving in `drop/` and
`done/` are refused (bead persistint-game-mxgq).

Why: the queued image prompts open "Kawaii pixel-art game art". The author rejected that style on
2026-11-04. The target is overhead Paper Mario: clean paper-doll characters, no pixel sprites
(bead persistint-game-tth2).

The hold ends when the style guide and the prompts are rewritten from the author's words and the
method is approved under SOP-17 (bead persistint-game-5uhv). The team then removes this file, and
the queue comes back.

Not affected: renders asked for on a bead with no prompt file (paper-doll sheets, host dolls), and
work that is not rendering.
