# char_player_mermaid

Character sheet: the player's mermaid base pose (the paper doll with a plain tail), turnaround and swim poses.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 character sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the character described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. The player's paper doll as a mermaid: the bald chibi paper doll (a big round head about half of her height with small round ears, two dot eyes, pink blush ovals and a small smile, a plain cream tank-top vest) from the waist down in one plain cream mermaid tail with a soft scallop pattern and a wide fin, arms slightly away from the body; bald, because hair, tops and tails are separate parts drawn over her; floating upright in the water as if standing, the tail gently curved. Chibi proportions on the same chibi body as the game's paper doll: a big round head about half the height, a short body, stubby limbs, dot eyes, pink blush ovals, a small curved smile, warm-brown outline. Character sheet laid out in two rows. Top row, a turnaround of the same figure, full body, all four views the same height with the fin tips on one shared baseline: front view, three-quarter front view, side view facing left, back view. Bottom row, five full-body poses of the same figure, all the same height: (1) floating upright with the tail gently curved, (2) mid-swim, leaning forward with the tail swept back, (3) a twirl with the tail curled round, (4) waving one hand, (5) blowing a ring of bubbles from cupped hands. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
