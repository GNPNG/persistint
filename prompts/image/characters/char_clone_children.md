# char_clone_children

Character sheet: the clone children, four batches, turnaround and expressions.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9 character sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the character described next, not this scene): Secret underground clone lab, spooky-cute: rows of glowing pink-fluid glass tanks, mint and pink hazard stripes, small pale-green shambling clone children with one X eye, stitches and goo drips, fluffy round white polar bear cubs, cartoon pink splatter with hearts and sparkles; Halloween-cartoon energy, nothing scary. The clone children of the bunker, small and kawaii, never scary: very pale cream-mint skin, a big round head, a hospital gown with a stitched seam, little legs, both arms stretched straight forward in a gentle shamble; every one has one sleepy dot eye and one X eye, pink blush, a tiny round mouth, forehead stitches and a pale goo drip off the chin. The turnaround shows one clone in a pink gown with grey swept-back hair; below it, also full body in front view, three more batches that share the same little face and differ only by hair and gown: white puffy hair and a round red nose in a sky-blue gown; a blonde bob in a lilac gown; brown side-parted hair with big round glasses in a butter-yellow gown. Chibi proportions on the same chibi body as the game's paper doll: a big round head about half the height, a short body, stubby limbs, dot eyes, pink blush ovals, a small curved smile, warm-brown outline. Character sheet laid out in two rows. Top row, a turnaround of the same figure, full body, all four views the same height with the feet on one shared baseline: front view, three-quarter front view, side view facing left, back view. Bottom row, five head-and-shoulders busts of the same character, all the same size: (1) a sleepy groan, mouth a small round o, (2) a hopeful look asking for a snack, (3) a happy little smile, (4) a confused tilt, (5) a yawn. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting (except the bunker's soft gloom), no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
