# char_reef_shark

Character sheet: the cheeky reef shark the player shoos off the nursery reef, turnaround and expressions.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 character sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the character described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. A friendly, cheeky Caribbean reef shark: a round chibi body in soft blue-grey with a pale cream belly, a rounded fin on its back, rounded side fins, a rounded tail, a rounded snout, round dot eyes with a white catch-light and sleepy eyelids, pink blush, a small closed-mouth smile with no teeth showing anywhere; sleepy and sweet, never fierce; the turnaround shows it swimming level. Chibi proportions in the game's critter style: a big round friendly head end, a short rounded body, stubby fins, dot eyes with a white catch-light, pink blush ovals, a small curved smile with the mouth closed and no teeth showing, warm-brown outline. Character sheet laid out in two rows.
