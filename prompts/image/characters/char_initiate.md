# char_initiate

Character sheet: the Initiate (cast id initiate), turnaround and expressions.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9 character sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the character described next, not this scene): Little tropical island village traced from a real island's shape: bunker-shaped pastel homes with smiling faces, bubbly mint, pink and lavender trees, bunting on vine-wrapped fences, a blue-and-white striped temple with a gold dome, a pastel cruise-ship wreck with vines on the south beach; mint grass, blush paving, sky-blue water; slightly soft, slightly desaturated pastels; every woman wears the same calm small smile; men in plain work clothes tend the gardens. The Initiate, a Devout Initiate of the island: a small, neat young woman with brown pigtails tied with pink ties, a sailor school uniform (white blouse, navy sailor collar, red neckerchief, black pleated skirt, white socks, black shoes), holding a closed book of sacred texts to her chest; bright eager smile. Chibi proportions on the same chibi body as the game's paper doll: a big round head about half the height, a short body, stubby limbs, dot eyes, pink blush ovals, a small curved smile, warm-brown outline. Character sheet laid out in two rows. Top row, a turnaround of the same figure, full body, all four views the same height with the feet on one shared baseline: front view, three-quarter front view, side view facing left, back view. Bottom row, five head-and-shoulders busts of the same character, all the same size: (1) bright eager smile, (2) eyes closed reciting, (3) the eager smile a little too fixed, (4) a polite blink, (5) a small proud smile holding the book up. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
