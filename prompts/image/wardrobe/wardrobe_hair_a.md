## Notes

Render 16:9 (the canon paper-doll sheet dev/art/canon-gemini/Gemini_Generated_Image_ktfw47ktfw47ktfw.jpg is 2752×1536; match it). The game: path is a staging sheet, not a sprite: the sheet is keyed (pure green to white or alpha, then despilled) and cut into parts on the shared 721×760 paper-doll frame (project/assets/canon/body_base.png: head top at y 61, chin about y 400, neck about y 410, feet at y 758, head centre x about 367) by the steps in .claude/skills/gemini-assets/SKILL.md. project/assets/sheets/ has a .gdignore and the Web preset excludes it, so a staged sheet is neither imported nor shipped. Parts on this sheet, left to right after the doll: hair_ponytail_honey, hair_buns_rose, hair_curls_cocoa. Slot: hair (in the PaperDoll stack order it is drawn over the dress and under the hat and the accessory). Register: each cut part in project/data/paperdoll_parts.json (this sheet under "sheets" with its path, each part with its "sheet" and its "crop" on it; cut the sheet with tools/cut_paperdoll.py --sheet ID) and in project/data/outfits.json under "hair"; then tools/gen_player_rig.py rigs it and tools/bake_paper.sh characters bakes every player rig. The cutter finds the face as the largest enclosed background region of each wig and scales the wig so that opening matches the body's face width, its bottom on the chin.
