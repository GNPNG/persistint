## Notes

Render 16:9 (the canon paper-doll sheet dev/art/canon-gemini/Gemini_Generated_Image_ktfw47ktfw47ktfw.jpg is 2752×1536; match it). The game: path is a staging sheet, not a sprite: the sheet is keyed (pure green to white or alpha, then despilled) and cut into parts on the shared 721×760 paper-doll frame (project/assets/canon/body_base.png: head top at y 61, chin about y 400, neck about y 410, feet at y 758, head centre x about 367) by the steps in .claude/skills/gemini-assets/SKILL.md. Godot imports everything under project/ and the export preset ships all resources, so project/assets/sheets/ needs a .gdignore or an export exclude before sheets land there (a config change for the lead to approve). Parts on this sheet, left to right after the doll: dress_pinafore_blush, dress_gingham_butter, dress_tiered_lavender. Slot: dress (the PaperDoll slot list in project/scripts/systems/paper_doll.gd is body, dress, hair, accessory). tools/cut_paperdoll.py fits a dress_* part from the collar (neck minus 14 px, y about 396) down to the soles, centred on the body, so each outfit must include its legwear and shoes, as the canon dress_uniform does. Register: each cut part in project/data/paperdoll_parts.json (crop on this sheet; the cutter takes one sheet per run, so a second sheet needs its own parts file or a cutter change) and in project/data/outfits.json under "dress"; add the part to the player list in tools/bake_paper.sh.
