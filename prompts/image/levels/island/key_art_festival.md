# key_art_festival

Key art for the Festival of the Fourteenth (cycle day 14 parade).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. A pastel parade on a sunny island: identical smiling young women in sailor uniforms in perfect lines, women in lavender robes leading, ribbons, garlands, offering baskets, bunting everywhere, petals and sparkles; joyful but eerily uniform. Key art for the Festival of the Fourteenth: high three-quarter view of a parade winding through the island village to the great hall, a blue-and-white striped pavilion temple with a gold dome on a terrace of blush-pink paving, with two Queen Mother icons flanking its door, each a smiling woman with a short blonde bob and pearls, a gold crown above, lavender jacket and pale-blue sash, in a white columned shrine frame, painted from memory with eyes slightly too large and too kind, and a red round Seal of the O (a red ring stamp with a bold O shape in the centre, no lettering) over the gate. At the front, Mothers in white gowns with pale-blue wraps, long wavy golden-blonde hair, small gold crowns and drop earrings, each cradling a sleeping swaddled baby, eyes closed in serene bliss, walking forward under a shower of petals while everyone praises them; behind them Priestesses in long lavender robes with light-brown braids and silver circlets lead with hands folded; then a perfectly even line of Initiates, young women in navy-and-white sailor school uniforms with a red neckerchief, black pleated skirt, white socks and black shoes, brown pigtails with pink ties, each in her good pastel sash and a flower garland, carrying an offering basket, all clapping on the same beat with the same small smile. At the back, Drones, men with messy brown hair in ragged grey-green tees, patched brown trousers and boots, mild and a little tired, and Barren women in tan headscarves, orange shirts and denim overalls carry baskets tied with ribbons. In the middle of the parade a little cart carries a smiling desk calendar with red spiral rings, a grid of pink and red squares, small red droplets, stars and crescent moons, no numbers. Along the route, homes shaped like the canon bunker: squat pastel concrete blocks with rounded doorways, smiling faces, bunting and ribbon bows, moss and bushes at the base and a pink flower growing on each roof; bunting on every vine-wrapped fence; bubbly mint, pink and lavender trees, each with a smiling face and sparkles; in the distance, the cruise-ship wreck aground on the south beach, sitting on sand and moss: pastel blue hull, pink upper deck, white superstructure with porthole rows, a pink funnel, vines climbing it, bunting strung from the mast, a smiling face on the bow. Bright pastel daylight, ribbons in every pastel, flower-pink accents, petals and sparkles in the air. Mood: joyful parade energy with a mechanical, too-perfect uniformity. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.
