# key_art_island_present

Key art for Little St. Piere Island village (island present).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Little tropical island village traced from a real island's shape: bunker-shaped homes that are squat pastel concrete blocks with rounded doorways, smiling faces, bunting, ribbon bows, moss and bushes at the base, and a pink flower growing on each roof; bubbly mint, pink, and lavender trees with smiling faces; pastel triangle bunting on vine-wrapped wooden fences; a blue-and-white striped temple pavilion with a gold dome on the hill, two Queen Mother icons flanking its door, each a smiling woman with a short blonde bob, pearls and a gold crown in a white shrine frame, and a red round Seal of the O (a red ring stamp with a bold O shape, no lettering) above the gate; the cruise-ship wreck grounded on the south beach, with a pastel blue hull, a pink upper deck, vines, bunting and a smiling bow face; mint clover grass, blush-pink paving with little hearts, sky-blue water. Chibi young women in navy-and-white sailor uniforms with red neckerchiefs, black pleated skirts and brown pigtails with pink ties carry offering baskets in an orderly line alongside tall women in lavender robes with braided hair and circlets, every woman wearing the exact same calm small smile, while plain men with messy brown hair in identical ragged grey-green tees and patched brown trousers tend tomatoes and seedlings inside a fenced garden; hearts float over the homes and sparkles over the temple and the wreck. Soft golden-hour pastel light, Animal Crossing scale, cozy, polite, and eerily uniform. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.

## Notes

16:9, 1920×1080 (shown at 640×360). No text; type is added in-engine.
