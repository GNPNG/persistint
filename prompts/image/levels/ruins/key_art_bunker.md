# key_art_bunker

Key art for the secret bunker clone lab.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even plum-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, straight-on symmetrical interior view, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Secret underground clone lab, spooky-cute: rows of four tall glass tanks filled with glowing pink fluid and rising bubbles (with a yellow rubber duck floating in the third tank), pink and mint pastel hazard stripes on the walls, warm incubators and lab machines decorated with tiny kawaii cat-in-a-lab-coat stickers. Small pale-green shambling chibi clone children with big round heads, one sleepy dot eye and one X eye, pink cheek blush, a tiny round groaning mouth, forehead stitches, a green goo drip on the chin, arms stretched straight out, wearing stitched hospital gowns in pastel pink, sky blue, lilac, and butter yellow (with swept-back grey hair, puffy white hair with a round red nose, a blonde bob, or side-parted brown hair with round glasses). Fluffy round white polar bear cubs with shiny dot eyes and pink inner ears pounce happily on a clone child in a burst of kawaii pastel-pink cartoon splatter, floating pink hearts, four-point sparkles, a white cartoon bone, and a worried little pink chunk with a blue sweat drop, while another cub naps in a pink basket with closed curved eyes and a pink-smeared muzzle. Nobody shows fear or pain; the clones look mildly puzzled. Dim blue-green lab gloom with soft pink tank glow, Halloween-cartoon splatter-comedy energy, nothing scary. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting (except the bunker's soft gloom), no muddy colours.

## Notes

16:9, 1920×1080 (shown at 640×360). No text; type is added in-engine.
