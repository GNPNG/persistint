# key_art_ruins

Key art for the Ruins of Utopia (wreck fused with bunker infrastructure).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Ruins of a luxury cruise ship fused with retro sci-fi bunker machinery: tilted pastel promenade decks, a striped deck chair facing a lost sunset, cute tilted slot machines and a cruise life ring with smiling faces, ceiling pipes painted with a pale red stripe, and humming steel-blue server racks with a control-desk altar; pastel pink and mint sacred ribbons braided around machine cables and tied around the deck chair and slot machines, a forest of lit candles on both the cruise relics and the control desks, wax running down into the vents, small cat-in-a-lab-coat stickers on every machine, and a big pink round O stencil painted above a floor hatch, with an arrow pointing down and no lettering. Overgrown vines, soft blue-green and sandstone gloom with pink and mint accents, hushed, candle-lit decayed glamour and absurd devotion. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting (except the bunker's soft gloom), no muddy colours.

## Notes

16:9, 1920×1080 (shown at 640×360). No text; type is added in-engine.
