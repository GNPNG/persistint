# key_art_landing

Key art for The Landing (bridge panic as the dead ship comes down).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. The same cruise ship dead in thick pale fog, deck lights failing, crew rushing between absurd control panels, deck chairs sliding, comedic chaos, no one hurt. Key art for The Landing: low angle on the bridge and deck of the same glamorous white cruise ship, now dead and drifting through thick pale grey-white fog toward the dim green silhouette of a small hilly island. Deck lights flicker and fail; pastel control panels with big round buttons, dials and knobs flash, each panel with a worried little face; a round hamster runs in a tiny wheel on a console; a big brass gong waits to be struck; one dial knob is turned past its last mark. Crew in white uniforms rush between the panels, one waving at the fog, others looking busy; a first mate calls out across the bridge; a telegenic cruise captain in a white dress uniform with gold epaulettes grips the wheel, trying not to crash. Deck chairs slide across the tilting deck, streamers and balloons droop in the fog, the ship's bow face looks worried. Dimmed glamour: gold trim muted by fog, cold grey light, flashing panel lights as the only accents. Mood: comedic chaos, panic, absurd, funny not scary; no one is hurt. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.
