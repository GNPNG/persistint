# undersea_props_seagrass

Props sheet: seagrass, mangrove roots and seagrass-flat things (magenta key, real greens).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the props described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Props sheet: each prop drawn alone, upright, seen slightly from above like the game's map props, its base flat at the bottom, the props in two rows, all at one scale; the big friendly props have small smiling faces. Seven props, in their real colours: (1) a clump of turtle grass: wide flat fresh-green ribbon blades; (2) a clump of manatee grass: thin round noodle-like green blades; (3) red mangrove roots: brown arching prop roots coming down from the top of the prop, a few small green leaves at the very top, two tiny fish among the roots; (4) a sheet of ruffled bright green sea lettuce on a pebble; (5) a merman's shaving brush: a green brush-shaped tuft on a short pale stalk; (6) a living queen conch on the sand, its shell cream and warm orange, two eyes on long stalks peeking out with a smile; (7) a puffy orange cushion sea star with a sweet face. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key magenta (hex FF00FF: red 255, green 0, blue 255) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no magenta tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is magenta, hot pink, purple or violet (the small pale cheek blush stays pale and soft); wherever a fin, spot or flower would be pink or purple, use orange, coral orange or butter yellow instead. Greens are welcome on this sheet. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the magenta. Generous empty space: the plain magenta shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no magenta, hot pink, purple or violet in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
