# key_art_florida

Key art for the Tropical Florida Keys.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, eye-level shoreline view with open horizon, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white wave foam, warm sand with colourful seashells, a friendly fisherman father and his young daughter in a sun hat skipping stones by a wooden dock, leaping dolphins, sideways-walking coral crabs, hopping seagulls, and two friendly aquarium-show mermaids waving from a rock amid floating bubbles; a sunny garden of pink hibiscus, magenta bougainvillea, plumeria, mango, and key-lime trees beside a little wooden crafting bench with a flower crown and shell garland. Full tropical sunshine, warm saturated turquoise, coral, hibiscus pink, mango, and key-lime green palette. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.

## Notes

16:9, 1920×1080 (shown at 640×360). No text; type is added in-engine.
