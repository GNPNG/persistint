# undersea_props_city

Props sheet: the bubble-dome city (domes, the bubble lift, the shell bench and mirror, lamps, the tank ladder).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the props described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Props sheet: each prop drawn alone, upright, seen slightly from above like the game's map props, its base flat at the bottom, the props in two rows, all at one scale; the big friendly props have small smiling faces. Eight props: (1) a small bubble-dome house, see-through pale pearl white with a soft blush glow inside drawn within its outline, a round door and a smiling face on the dome; (2) a short passage of joined round bubbles standing on a coral-pink base; (3) the bubble lift: a ring of cream shells on a patch of sand with a column of rising bubbles above it; (4) the shell bench: a big open scallop shell as a table on a coral-stone foot, with a smiling face; (5) the shell mirror: a tall oval mirror in a frame of spiral shells and pearls, the glass a soft pale-blue shine; (6) a pearl lamp: a coral-pink stalk with a softly glowing pearl on top; (7) a short ladder of honey-coloured rope with cream shell rungs, for the edge of a tank; (8) a patch of soft lilac sea anemones with a little orange-and-white striped fish peeking out. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
