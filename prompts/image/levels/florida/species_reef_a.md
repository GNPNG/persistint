# species_reef_a

Species sticker sheet A: eight reef fish of the Keys (phase 1 of the fish system).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the animals described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Species sticker sheet of eight real reef animals, sixteen stickers in four rows of four: each row holds two animals, and each animal is shown as a pair side by side, first gliding with its tail straight, then the same animal mid-stroke with its tail swept to one side and its side fin raised in a little wave. Every fish faces right, seen exactly from the side (a ray is seen from above, nose to the right); every animal is drawn the same length so it fills its place on the sheet; each has a cute face: a round dot eye with a white catch-light, a pale blush oval, a small closed-mouth smile with no teeth showing. Draw each animal's real shape, colours and markings faithfully, in the sticker style, in this order:  (1) a queen angelfish: a tall disc-shaped angelfish, electric blue with golden-yellow scale edges, a bright golden-yellow tail, top and bottom fins edged in bright blue that trail back into long points, yellow side fins, and on its forehead a dark blue crown-shaped spot ringed in bright blue; (2) a blue tang: an oval, flat surgeonfish, soft royal blue all over with thin darker blue lines along its body, pale-blue edges on its fins and a small pale mark by the tail; (3) a sergeant major: an oval damselfish, white and silver with a pale-yellow back and five dark-navy stripes from top to bottom; (4) a French grunt: an oval grunt with a silvery-blue body covered in bright yellow stripes that slant on its lower sides, and yellow fins; (5) a yellowtail snapper: a slim snapper, blue-grey and silver with small yellow spots on its upper back, a bright yellow stripe from its snout to its tail, and a deeply forked bright yellow tail; (6) a foureye butterflyfish: a small disc-shaped butterflyfish, pale silver-white with thin dark slanting lines, a dark bar running down through its dot eye, a big round dark eyespot ringed in white near its tail, and pale-yellow fins; (7) a porkfish: a deep-bodied grunt, silvery yellow with bright yellow stripes along its back half, two slanting dark-navy bars on its head (one through the eye, one behind the gill), and yellow fins; (8) a royal gramma: a small slender fish whose front half is bright violet and back half bright golden yellow, the two colours meeting in the middle, with a small dark spot at the front of its top fin. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
