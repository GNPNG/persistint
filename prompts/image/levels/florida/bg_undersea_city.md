# bg_undersea_city

Undersea map backdrop: the bubble-dome city, the hub under the aquarium.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style (the image shows the undersea place described next, under the turquoise Keys water, not the beach): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. A city under the sea: glowing, see-through bubble domes of many sizes ring a round sandy plaza, each dome softly lit from inside in pearl white and blush with a smiling face on it (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile), joined to each other by round passages made of joined bubbles; walls and dome feet grown from coral-pink and shell-cream rounded shapes, spiral shells and small glowing pearl lamps; a ring of cream shell paving in the plaza; at the bottom edge a ring of shells on the sand with a column of bubbles rising from it toward the light above; an original design, organic and bubbly, like a sea creature's soft glow made into a town. Map backdrop for a 640×360 game, seen from the overhead three-quarter view of the game's other maps: the open sandy middle of the image is clear for swimming, the features sit around the edges, and gaps in the scenery at the left, right and top edges and the ring of shells at the bottom show the ways out, with no signs, no arrows and no words. Light and colour: bright warm turquoise water, sun shafts from above, the domes glowing pearl white and blush from inside; the warmest undersea place. Sunshine streams down through the water in soft shafts, little fish with happy faces swim about, soft sea flowers and anemones dot the sand, sparkles and tiny bubbles drift; the big friendly things have smiling faces. Mood: tropical, sunny, carefree, curious, kind. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no sharp teeth, no menace, nothing broken or spooky, no signs or arrows, no ships other than the one described, no brand or film designs.
