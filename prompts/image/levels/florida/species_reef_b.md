Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the animals described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Species sticker sheet of eight real reef animals, sixteen stickers in four rows of four: each row holds two animals, and each animal is shown as a pair side by side, first gliding with its tail straight, then the same animal mid-stroke with its tail swept to one side and its side fin raised in a little wave. Every fish faces right, seen exactly from the side (a ray is seen from above, nose to the right); every animal is drawn the same length so it fills its place on the sheet; each has a cute face: a round dot eye with a white catch-light, a pale blush oval, a small closed-mouth smile with no teeth showing. Draw each animal's real shape, colours and markings faithfully, in the sticker style, in this order:  (1) a stoplight parrotfish (the big male): a sturdy parrotfish, bright sea green with orange-yellow lines on its head, a small bright-yellow spot at the top of its gill cover, a yellow bar at the base of its tail, and a beak of fused teeth; (2) a queen parrotfish (the big male): a sturdy parrotfish, blue-green with orange-yellow edges on its scales and fins and orange and green lines around its mouth; (3) a rainbow parrotfish: a big parrotfish with a bronze-orange head and front, a bright green back half and green fins, and a blue-green beak; (4) a midnight parrotfish: a sturdy parrotfish, deep dark blue all over with bright light-blue patches on its head and fins; (5) a blue parrotfish: a sturdy parrotfish, pale powder blue all over with a rounded bump on the front of its head and a pale-yellow patch on top of its head; (6) a bluehead (the big male): a slim wrasse with a bright blue head, then two dark-navy bands with a pale-blue band between them, and a green body and tail; (7) a yellowhead wrasse (the big male): a slim wrasse with a golden-yellow head, a blue-green body and a dark bar behind its head; (8) a green moray: a long eel, soft green all over with a long fin along its back and a rounded head, drawn in a gentle S-curve that fills the same space as the other fish (its stroke pose curves the other way).
