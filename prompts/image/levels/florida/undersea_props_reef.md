# undersea_props_reef

Props sheet: Keys corals and reef things for the reef zone.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the props described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Props sheet: each prop drawn alone, upright, seen slightly from above like the game's map props, its base flat at the bottom, the props in two rows, all at one scale; the big friendly props have small smiling faces. Seven props, each after the real coral's shape and colour: (1) elkhorn coral: wide, flat golden-ochre branches spreading like antlers, paler tips; (2) staghorn coral: a thicket of round honey-brown branches like deer antlers with pale tips; (3) a symmetrical brain coral: a round tan-ochre dome covered in winding grooves and ridges, with a smiling face; (4) a great star coral boulder: honey-brown, dotted all over with round star-shaped polyps; (5) pillar coral: three tall upright cream pillars with a soft fuzzy surface; (6) a common sea fan: a lacy lilac fan on a short stalk; (7) a long-spined sea urchin: a small round body with long thin dark-navy spines, a sweet face; no coral is ever broken or picked. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
