# undersea_props_caves

Props sheet: cave arches, the stones that glow, pearls and cave dwellers.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the props described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Props sheet: each prop drawn alone, upright, seen slightly from above like the game's map props, its base flat at the bottom, the props in two rows, all at one scale; the big friendly props have small smiling faces. Eight props: (1) a cave arch of smooth honey-cream rock with a friendly face; (2) a rounded rock pillar; (3) a cluster of small smooth stones that glow pale blue, lilac and gold, each glowing softly inside its own outline (the glow never spills onto the background); (4) an empty open oyster shell holding one white pearl; (5) a flat rock ledge with a tan-brown nurse shark asleep on it, eyes closed, a tiny snore bubble, mouth closed; (6) a cluster of butter-yellow tube sponges; (7) a patch of rock with a soft round pale-blue glow drawn inside its outline; (8) a crack in a rock with a spiny lobster peeking out, long antennae, honey and cream with pale spots. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
