# species_reef_c

Species sticker sheet C: odd-shaped reef fish (triggerfish, puffers, boxfish, trumpetfish, filefish, seahorse).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Pure carefree joy, nothing wrong anywhere. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the animals described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Species sticker sheet of eight real reef animals, sixteen stickers in four rows of four: each row holds two animals, and each animal is shown as a pair side by side, first gliding with its tail straight, then the same animal mid-stroke with its tail swept to one side and its side fin raised in a little wave. Every fish faces right, seen exactly from the side (a ray is seen from above, nose to the right); every animal is drawn the same length so it fills its place on the sheet; each has a cute face: a round dot eye with a white catch-light, a pale blush oval, a small closed-mouth smile with no teeth showing. Draw each animal's real shape, colours and markings faithfully, in the sticker style, in this order:  (1) a queen triggerfish: a diamond-shaped triggerfish, golden yellow and soft lilac-grey, with two bright blue lines curving across its face and long trailing tips on its top fin, bottom fin and tail; (2) a porcupinefish: an oval pufferfish, sandy tan with small dark spots, its spines lying flat along its body, and big round eyes; (3) a spotted trunkfish: a small box-shaped fish, dark charcoal-navy with white spots all over, a tiny mouth and little fins; (4) a honeycomb cowfish: a box-shaped fish with two little horns above its eyes, butter yellow with a blue honeycomb pattern of six-sided shapes; (5) a sharpnose puffer: a tiny round puffer with a brown back covered in small blue spots, a pale orange-and-white belly and a pointed snout; (6) a trumpetfish: a long thin fish with a trumpet-shaped snout, honey brown with pale stripes and small dark spots, drawn straight; (7) a scrawled filefish: a long, flat filefish in warm brown with bright blue scribbles and small dark spots, and a broom-like tail; (8) a lined seahorse: an upright seahorse in honey orange with thin white lines, its tail curled, the gliding pose upright and the stroke pose leaning forward with its little back fin fluttering. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.
