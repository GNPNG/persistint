World for colour and style only (the image shows just the animals described next, not this scene): Sunny tropical Florida Keys: turquoise shallows, white foam, sand and shells, hibiscus, bougainvillea, key lime and mango trees, leaping dolphins, sideways crabs, hopping gulls, friendly mermaids; warm saturated colours, pure joy. Species sticker sheet of eight real reef animals, sixteen stickers in four rows of four: each row holds two animals, and each animal is shown as a pair side by side, first gliding with its tail straight, then the same animal mid-stroke with its tail swept to one side and its side fin raised in a little wave.
