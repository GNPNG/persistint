# key_art_temple

Key art for the Temple of the Queen Mothers (interior).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Symmetrical pastel temple interior: cream and blush checkerboard floor, pink runner to a dais, a row of identical portrait icons of a smiling blonde woman with a crown on the high wall, a red round seal with a bold O above the dais, white columns topped with smiling orbs, a cute flower-crowned skull on a pedestal; gauzy white-pink light. Key art for the Temple of the Queen Mothers: straight-on, perfectly symmetrical interior view centred on a pink dais. High cream brick walls with a pale cap; a cream and blush-pink checkerboard floor; a pink rug runner with pale-gold borders and pale hearts running from the door up to the dais. Along the high back wall, six identical Queen Mother icons in a row, three on each side of the dais, each a smiling woman with a short blonde bob and pearls, a gold crown above, lavender jacket and pale-blue sash, in a white columned shrine frame, painted from memory with eyes slightly too large and too kind. Above the dais, a red round Seal of the O (a red ring stamp with a bold O shape in the centre, no lettering). A colonnade of four pillar-orbs on each side, each a white column topped with a pale orb that has a smiling face. On the wall, a smiling desk calendar with red spiral rings, a grid of pink and red squares, little red droplets, stars and crescent moons, no numbers. On the east side a round ritual table with the skull altar: a cute white cartoon skull on a round pedestal, crowned with pink flowers and a red bow, sparkles around it. On the west wall a sealed alcove door with a red luggage tag marked with a red X in a circle. A Priestess in a long lavender robe with wide sleeves, light-brown braids and a silver circlet with a jewel, standing very straight with hands folded, before the dais with the same small calm smile; two Initiates, young women in navy-and-white sailor school uniforms with a red neckerchief, black pleated skirt, white socks and black shoes, brown pigtails with pink ties, kneel on the runner holding closed books to their chests. Bright, even, white-pink light like sunlight through gauze; cream, blush and lavender with the gold of the crowns as the warmest note and the seal's red as the accent; sparkles drift. Mood: reverent, pastel, ceremonial, hushed, symmetrical, faintly oppressive. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.
