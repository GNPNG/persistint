# temple_tiles_sheet

Temple floor and wall tile sheet (source for the temple cells of the island tile atlas).

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9 asset sheet. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. World for colour and style only (the image shows just the tiles described next, not this scene): Symmetrical pastel temple interior: cream and blush checkerboard floor, pink runner to a dais, a row of identical portrait icons of a smiling blonde woman with a crown on the high wall, a red round seal with a bold O above the dais, white columns topped with smiling orbs, a cute flower-crowned skull on a pedestal; gauzy white-pink light. Tile sheet for the temple interior, each tile a perfect square, all tiles the same size, laid out in a neat grid of two rows of four with wide gaps between them, every tile flat and seen straight from above, edges that repeat seamlessly when tiled: (1) a cream polished marble floor slab with a soft highlight and a pale-gold diamond inlay at each corner; (2) the same slab in blush pink; (3) a section of pink rug runner with pale-gold borders on both sides and one pale heart, the floor not showing; (4) the fringed end of the pink rug runner; (5) cream brick wall face in soft staggered blocks with a light top edge on each block; (6) the top of the wall: a pale cap stone with a pale-gold band and a pink scalloped frieze; (7) the foot of the wall: blush wainscot panels under a pale-gold rail; (8) the top of the pink dais step with a pale-gold edge. No faces on the tiles. Background: one perfectly flat, solid, pure chroma-key green (hex 00FF00: red 0, green 255, blue 0) filling the whole image edge to edge, with no shadows, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor or ground line, no glow and no green tint spilling onto anything. Nothing in any item is green, lime or mint; wherever a leaf or plant would be green, use soft lavender, pink or butter yellow instead. Every item keeps its complete warm-brown outline all the way round, so it separates cleanly from the green. Generous empty space: the plain green shows between all items, with a gap of at least half an item's width between neighbours and from every edge of the image; no item touches, overlaps or is cut off by another item or the edge. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours, no scenery or backdrop, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no gradient, textured or patterned background, no green, lime or mint in any item, no item touching another item or the image edge, no cropped items.

## Notes

The temple floor, rug, dais and walls are cells of the generated atlas project/assets/tiles/island_tiles.png (128×128 file px per 32 px map cell; tools/gen_island_tiles.py, then tools/build_island_maps.gd), so no render replaces them by sync. This sheet lands in a staging path: each tile is keyed, resized to 128×128 and wired into tools/gen_island_tiles.py by hand (a tool change), then the atlas and maps are rebuilt. Godot imports everything under project/, so project/assets/sheets/ needs a .gdignore or an export exclude.
