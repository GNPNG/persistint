# temple_altar

Ritual puzzle backdrop: the temple's altar wall behind the icon-arranging panel.

## Prompt

Kawaii pixel-art game art in a clean sticker-illustration style: even warm-brown outlines (never black), flat pastel fills with one soft highlight, round pink cheek blush, dot eyes and small smiles, cute faces (two dot eyes, pink blush, a small smile) on buildings, trees, props, the ship and the bunker, big round readable shapes for a 640×360 game, overhead three-quarter view for places, hearts and four-point sparkles as motifs, soft antialiased edges with no harsh pixel stair-steps. Cute on the surface with something faintly wrong underneath. 16:9. No photorealism, no 3D, no text. Symmetrical pastel temple interior: cream and blush checkerboard floor, pink runner to a dais, a row of identical portrait icons of a smiling blonde woman with a crown on the high wall, a red round seal with a bold O above the dais, white columns topped with smiling orbs, a cute flower-crowned skull on a pedestal; gauzy white-pink light. Backdrop for the ritual puzzle, straight-on and perfectly symmetrical: the high back wall of the temple behind the dais, soft blue-and-white vertical stripes under a pale-gold cornice with a pink scalloped frieze, the red round Seal of the O (a red ring with a bold O, no lettering) high in the centre, three Queen Mother icons on each side (a smiling woman with a short blonde bob and pearls, a gold crown above, lavender jacket and pale-blue sash, in a white columned shrine frame, eyes slightly too large and too kind), a pillar-orb with a smiling orb at each side, cream candles with soft flames, the top step of the pink dais and a strip of cream and blush checkerboard floor along the bottom, a pink rug runner edge, a cute flower-crowned skull on a pedestal at one side. The middle of the image is calm and uncluttered (soft stripes only) because the puzzle panel covers it. Bright, even, white-pink light like sunlight through gauze; cream, blush and lavender with the gold as the warmest note and the seal's red as the accent; sparkles drift. Mood: reverent, pastel, ceremonial, hushed, symmetrical, faintly oppressive. Negative prompt: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no photorealism, no 3D render, no black outlines, no harsh pixel stair-stepping, no dithering, no grime, no realistic blood, no wounds, no weapons, no fighting, no scary faces, no nudity, no anatomy, no real-person likeness, no dark horror lighting, no muddy colours.

## Notes

16:9, render at 2560×1440 or larger and resize to 2560×1440 (4× the 640×360 view; the ritual puzzle scales it to cover the screen). No chroma key: full-frame art. Replaces the tools/gen_ui_art.py stand-in with no code change.
